FAQ

Questions parents ask first

Does it read all my emails?

No. If you connect Gmail or Outlook, SchoolSphere only reads emails from senders you've added to your trusted list. It never touches personal or unrelated emails.

Is my data safe?

Email content is never stored — it's read, extracted, and discarded immediately. Uploaded documents are deleted the moment processing is complete. We're UK GDPR compliant.

Which email providers are supported?

Gmail and Outlook connect directly via OAuth. Any other provider — iCloud, Yahoo, BT, Sky — works through email forwarding.

Can my partner or a grandparent use it too?

Yes. Family Sharing lets you invite a partner, grandparent or carer. You control whether they have full access or view-only.

What if the AI gets a date wrong?

Every extracted event is shown to you for review before it's added. You can edit any detail — date, time, title, requirements — before confirming.

What schools does it work with?

Any school. SchoolSphere reads whatever you share with it — it isn't tied to a particular school system, so it works the same everywhere.
