Common questions

Most issues fall into one of these.

My school emails aren't turning into events

Check these in order:

  1. Open Settings → Connected accounts and confirm your mailbox still shows as connected. Providers occasionally revoke access after a password change.
  2. Confirm the school actually emails the address you connected. Many parents receive school mail at a different address to the one they signed up with.
  3. Give it a few minutes — new mail is processed shortly after it arrives, not instantly.

Still nothing? Email us the date and subject line of an email that should have produced an event and we'll trace it.

How do I connect my Gmail, Outlook or iCloud mailbox?

Go to Settings → Connected accounts → Add mailbox. For Gmail or Outlook, sign in with your provider and grant read access. For iCloud, generate an app-specific password at appleid.apple.com and enter it — SchoolSphere connects directly over IMAP to look for school mail. All three connect directly; other providers use forwarding instead.

You can disconnect at any time from the same screen, and disconnecting stops all further processing immediately.

An event was added with the wrong date or child

Tap the event and edit it — corrections stick and won't be overwritten.

If a particular school or sender is consistently misread, send us an example. Extraction accuracy improves from real cases and it's the most useful thing you can send us.

How do I delete my account and my data?

In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account, your children's details, your events and any connected mailbox links.

If you can't access the app, email support@schoolsphere.app from your account address and we'll action it for you.

How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed on our website rather than in the app. Sign in at schoolsphere.co.uk/subscription to view your plan, update your payment details or cancel.

Cancelling stops future billing and you keep access until the end of the period you've already paid for.

What does SchoolSphere do with my emails?

Mail is read only to find school events, dates and deadlines — the AI provider keeps nothing. The text is saved with your account so events stay complete and can be re-checked, visible only to your household. We don't sell data and we don't use your mail for advertising.

The full detail is in our privacy policy.

Adding another parent or carer

Go to Family → Invite and send an invitation. They'll see the same calendar, so school dates stop living in one person's head.

Something's broken and it isn't listed here

Email support@schoolsphere.app with what you were doing, what you expected, and what happened instead. Your device model and iOS version help, but don't worry if you're not sure.

Still stuck?

Write to support@schoolsphere.app. There's no ticket queue and no chatbot — just tell us what's happening and we'll pick it up.
